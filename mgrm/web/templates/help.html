MenuWhat it's for
AgreementsEvery customer rebate agreement, by entity, customer and brand. Open one to see its rates and their history, its evidence, exactly what it covers, and its change log.

JournalsEach month's accrual journal for NetSuite: prepared, approved, and downloaded for import.
