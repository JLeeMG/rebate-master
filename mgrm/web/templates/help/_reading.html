Finding things

  • A customer's rates: Rate cards → choose the entity and the customer group → Show. Change As at to see the rates in force on another date.
  • A brand's rates across retailers: Rate cards → type the brand (e.g. EUFY) → Show.
  • Why a rate is what it is: open the agreement: each rate shows who entered it, why, the source, the evidence, and who approved it.
  • What changed recently: Change log, filtered by entity, person or date.
  • Agreements ending soon: Agreements → Expiry and renewal.
  • Evidence: click a file name to download it.
