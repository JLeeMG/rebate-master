Administering the platform

ToDo this
Add a personUsers → Add a person: name, work email, role, and either a temporary password (they choose their own at first sign-in) or Microsoft 365 → Add.
Change someone's roleUsers → choose the new role on their row → Save. They are signed out everywhere and sign in again with the new role.
Someone forgot their passwordUsers → type a temporary password on their row → Reset, and tell them it; they choose their own at next sign-in.
Someone leavesUsers → Remove access on their row. They are signed out at once; their history stays. Restore access undoes it.
A brand owner approves their brands (after the beta)Give them the role Brand rebate approver, then Agreements → Brand approvers → assign the brand. From then on only they approve that brand's proposals.
The forecasting platform's accessFeed tokens: create one (shown once), put it in the forecasting platform's .env, revoke the old one. Tokens last a year.
Check who did whatAudit log: every sign-in, refused page, user change and data load, with the network address.
