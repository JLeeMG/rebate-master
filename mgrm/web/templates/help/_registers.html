Customers, customer groups and brands

This platform is the master of these registers; the forecasting platform copies them from here.

  • Find customers: Customers → type part of the name in Name or parent contains (e.g. "HN Townsville"), or tick Only customers without a group → Show.
  • Put one in a group: choose the group on its row and click Save.
  • Put every customer shown in a group: after searching, use Put all … customers matching … into: choose the group, give a reason, and click Assign all. Check the list first.
  • A new group: Add a customer group near the top: a short code, the name and a reason. Give a retailer its own group when it has its own rebate agreement.
  • Brands: every NetSuite class. Tick Brand? for the ones that are brands (not e.g. 3PL or Freight), set the tier, give a reason, and click Save.
  • These changes take effect at once and are recorded in the audit log with your reason; they don't need a second person.
  • The forecasting platform picks them up when someone there clicks Refresh from the Rebate Master.

Brand owners and review packs

Each brand's owner (the Group Product Manager, or whoever holds that responsibility, e.g. the Product Manager Lead - NZ) checks the rebate agreements on their brands, in Excel. Packs and screens use each person's own title. This gives them no access to the platform.

  1. Owners: keep the Brand owners workbook (brand code, the owner's and the PM's names and emails, the owner's title, notes) and load it under Load data → Brand owners. Brands → Brand owners and review packs shows who has what.

  2. Send: Brands → Download review pack for each brand owner, and email it to them, copying the PMs listed. The pack lists every agreement on their brands, including retailer-wide ones that cover them: the retailer, what it covers, the type, the rate in force and its start date, and their brands' sales at that retailer over the last 12 months. Finance's notes, sources and journal details are not in it.
  3. They answer three yellow columns: Correct? (Y/N), Correct rate (%) and Comment, and email it back.
  4. Load the reply: Load data → A returned brand review pack. Confirmations are recorded against each agreement; corrections and comments are listed on the Load data page, and the returned workbook is attached as evidence to those agreements.
  5. Act on corrections as ordinary proposals on the agreement (the owner's workbook is already there as evidence); someone else approves them.
