Customers, customer groups and brands

This platform is the master of these registers; the forecasting platform copies them from here.

  • Find customers: Customers → type part of the name in Name or parent contains (e.g. "HN Townsville"), or tick Only customers without a group → Show.
  • Put one in a group: choose the group on its row and click Save.
  • Put every customer shown in a group: after searching, use Put all … customers matching … into: choose the group, give a reason, and click Assign all. Check the list first.
  • A new group: Add a customer group near the top: a short code, the name and a reason. Give a retailer its own group when it has its own rebate agreement.
  • Brands: every NetSuite class. Tick Brand? for the ones that are brands (not e.g. 3PL or Freight), set the tier, give a reason, and click Save.
  • These changes take effect at once and are recorded in the audit log with your reason; they don't need a second person.
  • The forecasting platform picks them up when someone there clicks Refresh from the Rebate Master.
