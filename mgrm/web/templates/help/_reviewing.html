Reviewing someone else's proposal

{% if shown == Role.BRAND_APPROVER %}

You approve rate changes for the brands the administrator has assigned to you. Proposals for other brands show in Review with the reason you cannot decide on them. You see rates but not sales figures.

{% elif shown == Role.REBATE_MAINTAINER %}

You and the other rebate editor and approver review each other's entries. You never see an Approve button on your own. If a brand has an assigned approver (e.g. its Group Product Manager), only they can approve that brand's proposals.

{% else %}

You approve or reject what others enter. If a brand has an assigned approver (e.g. its Group Product Manager), only they can approve that brand's proposals.

{% endif %}
  1. Open Review. Rates and new agreements come first; changes to agreements (details, what it covers, endings) below.
  2. For each one, open the evidence and check the rate, the dates, the customer and the brand against it. For a change to what an agreement covers, check the brands and stores listed against the trading terms.
  3. Approve, or Reject with a note saying why (a note is required, so the person who entered it can correct it).
  4. Several rates checked against their sources can be approved together: tick them, tick I have checked each selected rate against its source, and click Approve selected.

A wrong rate here is wrong in every accrual and forecast after it. If anything doesn't match the evidence, reject it with a note rather than approving and fixing later.
