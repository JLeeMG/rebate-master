For everyone

  • Signing in: with your platform password, or the Microsoft button if the administrator set you up for Microsoft 365.
  • Your password: at least 12 characters (a short phrase is easiest). Change it from Password at the top right. If you forget it, ask the administrator to reset it.
  • Five wrong passwords from your computer make it wait 15 minutes.
  • You are signed out after 8 hours without a click, 12 hours after signing in however busy, and everywhere at once when you sign out, change your password, or your role changes.
  • "Form expired" means the page was open too long or you signed in elsewhere: go back, refresh, and do it again. Nothing was changed.
  • "Not available to your role" means your role doesn't include that page; ask the administrator if you need it. The attempt is recorded.

Words used here

AgreementOne rebate arrangement: entity × customer group × brand or products × type (rebate, damage, settlement, co-op, MDF). Several can apply to the same sales.
ProposalAnything entered: it has no effect until someone else approves it.
Four eyesTwo different people for every change: one enters, another approves.
What it covers (scope)Exactly which brands and stores an agreement's rate applies to, and the customer its journal lines are tagged with.
Check onlyAn agreement worked out for checking the retailer's claim but not accrued.
EvidenceThe document behind a rate: kept permanently, exactly as uploaded.
